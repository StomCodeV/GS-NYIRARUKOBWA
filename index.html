Our History & Legacy

A journey of excellence since 1974

📅 School Timeline

1974 École Primaire Nyirarukobwa was founded
2013 Airtel Rwanda renovated the school with a 20 million Franc investment — covering roofs of 11 classrooms, doors, windows, benches for 200 students, three water tanks, and toilet construction
2013 ISOC Rwanda Chapter provided a $10,000 grant to bring connectivity to the school, purchasing five computers and setting up a computer lab
2020 First Safer Internet Day celebrated at G.S Nyirarukobwa
2021 Smart Teacher Project launched — 10 teachers received smartphones and training
2024 Global Encryption Day celebrated at the school
2024 Safer Internet Day 2024 hosted at G.S Nyirarukobwa with theme "Promoting personal accountability for a constructive internet atmosphere"
2025 Digital Parenting Training held at the school

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Where is the school located?
Ntarama Sector, Bugesera District, Eastern Province, Rwanda. View on map →
What levels does the school offer?
Nursery, Lower Primary, Upper Primary, and O-Level.
How can I apply?
Visit our Apply section above to complete the online application form. You'll need the student's SDMS Code.
What documents are needed for admission?
Birth certificate, previous school report, medical records, parent/guardian identification, and SDMS Code.
How can I contact the school?
You can visit the school at Ntarama Sector, Bugesera District, or use the contact section below.
What is SDMS and why do I need it?
SDMS (School Data Management System) is a system by the Ministry of Education to track student records. It's required for all students in Rwanda. Learn more →
